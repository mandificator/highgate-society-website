[White background. Left-aligned.]

### Section label
`THE REALITY`

### Heading
## AI doesn't replace people. It replaces what people used to be paid to do.

Parts of your work — analysis, writing, research, first drafts, pattern recognition — are now done faster and cheaper by machines. That's not a future scenario. It's Tuesday.

The question isn't whether AI affects your role. It does. The question is: what do you do with the parts that are still yours? What becomes more valuable now? What do you let go of, delegate, or reinvent?

Most professionals are stuck between two bad responses: ignoring AI entirely, or chasing every new tool without a strategy.

There's a third option. Think clearly about it — with the right people in the room.

### Card 2

**The Transition Model**
William Bridges
*Author of Managing Transitions (1991)*

Change is external — it happens to you. Transition is internal — it's how you process it. Three phases: Ending, Neutral Zone, New Beginning. Most people try to skip the middle. The middle is where the real thinking happens.

*How we use it: we identify where you are psychologically — not just practically — so we meet you where you actually are.*

### Card 3

**Career Anchors**
Edgar H. Schein
*MIT Sloan School of Management*

Eight career motivators that stay stable across a lifetime. Your anchor is what you won't sacrifice — autonomy, expertise, impact, security, the drive to build. Identifying it explains what energises you, what you resist, and where your real leverage is.

*How we use it: we understand what drives you — so the path forward is yours, not a template.*

## 9. FAQ

[Accordion style.]

### Section label
`QUESTIONS`

### Heading
## What you'd want to know before applying.

---

**What actually happens in a Mastermind session?**
One or two members bring a real, current challenge — not a case study, their actual situation. The group and facilitator work through it: perspectives, hard questions, blind spots, options. It's structured, timed, and facilitated. Not a panel discussion. Not a networking event.

**Do I need to be technical or know about AI?**
No. This isn't a technology programme. We work with the human and strategic side — how AI affects your role, your team, your business model, your decisions. Some members are deeply technical. Others have never opened ChatGPT. Both benefit equally.

**I run a company. Is this relevant for founders?**
Yes. Founders face AI disruption differently than employees — it affects product, team structure, competitive positioning, and what you personally should be doing vs. delegating. The Mastermind gives you a space to think about that with people outside your investor circle and your team.

**How are group members selected?**
Online assessment, LinkedIn review, and a 15-minute screening conversation. We select for seniority fit, industry diversity, and willingness to be honest. If we don't think it's a fit, we say so.

**What if I miss a session?**
One missed session is manageable — you'll get a debrief. Two or more significantly reduces the value for you and the group. We ask for real commitment.

**Is this therapy?**
No. It's professional coaching in a peer group format. We work with high-functioning professionals navigating a specific external challenge. If we believe someone needs clinical support, we'll say so directly.

**What does it cost?**
€750 for the full six-week programme. Payment before the first session.

**What language are sessions in?**
English. Members join from across Europe and beyond.
